This policy describes what CLAWW ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. It's written against what the app actually does, not a generic template.

Who this covers

Anyone who creates a CLAWW account or uses the app.

What we collect

Account information

Email address and password, handled by Supabase Auth. We never see or store your raw password.

Profile information

Name, age, height, weight, gender, fitness goal, experience level, and available equipment.

Health and fitness data

A sensitive data category under Google Play's policies:

  • Body metrics: height, weight, age
  • Free-text injury or limitation descriptions, used to filter exercises
  • Sleep logs: hours, bedtime, wake time
  • Workout logs: exercises, sets, prescribed vs. achieved reps/weight
  • Meal and nutrition logs, including estimated calories and macros
  • Water intake logs

Photos

Meal photos you submit are sent for nutritional analysis; the resulting estimate (not the photo) is stored in your meal log.

Usage and gamification data

XP events, achievements, streaks, and daily AI-generation request counts (for fair-use limits).

What we don't collect

Precise location, contacts, or payment information — CLAWW doesn't currently process payments.

How we use it

  • Personalizing your plan. Rep ranges, training frequency, and injury filtering are computed deterministically — never by an AI model. An AI model only selects/names exercises within limits we've already computed, and estimates nutrition from text or photos. Every AI result is validated before being shown to you.
  • Tracking progress. Your logs power recovery scores, streaks, and the Tracker tab.
  • Fair use enforcement. We track daily generation requests to keep the service usable for everyone.
  • Operating the service. We log when and why an AI request failed (never your personal data) to help us fix reliability issues — this log is never shown to you in the app.
