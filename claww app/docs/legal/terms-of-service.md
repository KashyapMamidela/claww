## 2. What CLAWW is

CLAWW generates personalized workout and nutrition guidance from information
you provide (body metrics, goals, equipment, and — optionally — injuries or
limitations), tracks your logged activity, and adapts future plans based on
what you actually did. Exercise selection is constrained by rules we
compute, not left entirely to an automated model — see the Privacy Policy
for how that works.

## 3. Not medical advice

**CLAWW is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.**
Workout plans, nutrition estimates, and recovery scores are general-purpose
fitness guidance, not a substitute for a doctor, physical therapist, or
registered dietitian. The injury/limitation filter is a best-effort
heuristic, not a clinical contraindication database.

If you have an injury, a medical condition, or you're pregnant, talk to a
qualified professional before starting or changing any exercise or nutrition
program. Nutrition figures (calories, macros) — whether typed in or
estimated from a photo — are estimates and may be inaccurate; do not rely on
them for a medical condition requiring precise nutritional control (e.g.
diabetes) without professional guidance.
