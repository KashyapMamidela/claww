Delete Your CLAWW Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your CLAWW account and data, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

If you still have CLAWW installed and can sign in:

  1. Open CLAWW and go to Profile
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account
  3. Type DELETE to confirm

This deletes your account immediately — there's no waiting period.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you've uninstalled CLAWW, forgotten your password, or otherwise can't sign in, email us at privacy@claww.app from the email address associated with your account and include the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days, and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile (name, email, age, height, weight, goals, injury notes)
  • All workout plans and logged sets (prescribed and achieved)
  • All meal, sleep, and water logs, including any meal photos submitted for analysis
  • XP, streaks, and achievement history
  • Your authentication credentials
This action cannot be undone. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered by us or by you.

Retention

In-app deletions are processed immediately. Deletion requests submitted by email are processed within 30 days of verification. Backups that include your data are rotated out within our standard backup retention window after deletion.
