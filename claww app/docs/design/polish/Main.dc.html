CLAWW · plan redrafted 6 Nov 2026 · for the team

Polish the app we have. Fix what's wrong, keep what works.

No full redesign. The current look stays: the grain texture, the glow, a colour per feature. We fix the mistakes found on the phone, make onboarding start from your goal and how you train, and add the few ideas that save the most time.

fewer taps, clearer words, same soul

Decided

  • Polish, not redesign. Home's Today line is the one bigger change you approved.
  • Onboarding starts with your goal, then how you train: gym, calisthenics, home workout, yoga, mobility (pick more than one).
  • Burned becomes the outer ring; its goal comes from your calorie goal.
  • Keep and spread the texture and glow: one CLAWW colour for buttons and tabs, each feature its own soft glow.

Dropped

  • "From the home pot": too heavy for what it gives. "Save as my plate" covers home food instead.
  • "Move" as a path: confusing. Yoga and mobility are training styles under your goal, not a separate track.
  • The black and white draft screens and the new tab structure.

Good points kept from the research

  • Fewer taps wins: people log food 4 to 6 times a day.
  • Say yes, don't fill in: the app proposes, you tap once.
  • Show the change, not the plan: the pen is CLAWW's signature, use it only where something changed.
  • Never punish a bad day: missed meals and rest days get a kind one-tap answer.
  • Plain words a 12-year-old gets; Apple type sizes (34 / 22 / 17 / 15 / 13), buttons 50 and 44 tall.
  • Light on the phone: plain SVG rings and charts, one small grain image, no heavy blur.

Later, after the closed beta

  • One buddy: see a friend's ticks, one nudge a day.
  • Weekly streak ("4 good days this week").
  • Four tabs with a + button.
  • 3D body model (waits on your choice of model).
