Cut from the app

Diagonal hatch in hero corners, dot grids on tiles, watermark icons, the "✦ 775 CLAWW" pill on Home (XP moves to More), ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels over every card, the purple "CLAWW INSIGHT" bar card, the three trust chips and lock line on Welcome, "Privacy-Protected — your data stays private".

Kept and used everywhere

One film grain on every card and full screen (not only Home), a top light on cards, glow only on data (rings, the now node), one colour per area, Caveat only for pen notes, sentence-case section titles.

Burned goal

The activity part of your calorie target: BMR × (activity multiplier − 1.2), so a moderate 68 kg user gets about 410 kcal, active about 830; never under 250; editable in the Burned sheet. Burned is Health Connect active energy when connected, else the steps estimate plus logged sessions and activities.
