Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026

This policy explains what CLAWW collects on this website and in the CLAWW app, why we collect it, who helps us run the service, and how you can see, change or delete your data. The short version: we collect what we need to plan your training and track your progress, we never sell your data, and you can delete it whenever you like.

1. Who we are

CLAWW is a fitness app made in India. In this policy, “CLAWW”, “we” and “us” mean the team that builds and runs the CLAWW app and this website. You can reach us at crew@claww.me.

2. The website and waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we collect:

  • your name and email address
  • your city, if you choose to add it
  • which phone you use (Android, iPhone or both) and what you want from CLAWW, if you pick any
  • the time you joined, and a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address, used only to stop automated spam sign-ups

We use this to send you one confirmation email, to tell you when CLAWW launches on your phone, and to understand who is waiting for the app. We don’t use it for advertising and we don’t sell or rent it.

The website doesn’t use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to download them.

3. The CLAWW app

To plan your training and track your progress, the app collects:

Account Your email address, or your Google account name and email if you sign in with Google.
Profile Age, gender, height, weight, goals, training experience, available equipment, schedule and food preferences, as you enter them during setup.
Training Your plans, the sets, reps and weights you log, workout history, personal records and how sore or fresh you report your muscles.
Food Meals you log, including descriptions you type and photos you take of your food, and the calorie and nutrient estimates for them.
Daily activity Water and sleep you log, and steps and active calories from your phone or from Health Connect if you allow it.
Notifications A device token so we can send reminders you’ve turned on.
Usage and errors Which screens and features you use, and crash reports when something goes wrong, so we can fix problems and improve the app.

Meal photos are stored privately with your account. Only you can see them in the app.

4. Health Connect

On Android, CLAWW can read your steps and active calories burned from Health Connect, only after you give permission. We use them to show your daily activity and to plan your training. CLAWW doesn’t write to Health Connect, and you can remove its access at any time in Health Connect’s settings.

CLAWW’s use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We don’t use Health Connect data for advertising, and we don’t sell it or share it with anyone except to provide CLAWW’s features to you.

5. How we use your data

  • To build and adjust your workout plan, and to suggest meals that fit what you have left for the day.
  • To estimate calories and nutrients from the meals you describe or photograph.
  • To show your progress in the CLAWW Tracker.
  • To send reminders and the emails you’ve asked for.
  • To keep the service secure, fix bugs and improve features.

Some features use artificial intelligence. When you ask for a plan, a meal estimate or a meal suggestion, the details needed for that request (for example your goals and training history, or your meal photo or description) are sent to our AI provider to produce the result.

We never sell your personal data, and we don’t show ads.

6. Services that help us

We use a small number of trusted providers to run CLAWW. They process data only on our behalf:

Supabase Database, sign-in and private file storage for your account, logs and meal photos.
Groq AI processing for workout plans, meal estimates and meal suggestions.
Google Sign in with Google, Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications, Health Connect on your phone, and Google Fonts on this website.
PostHog Product analytics inside the app: which features are used.
Sentry Crash and error reports.
Vercel Hosting for this website.
Resend Sending waitlist and service emails.
Zoho Mail Our inbox, for the emails you send us.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside India. We may also share data if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

7. How long we keep it

  • Waitlist: until CLAWW has launched on your phone and we’ve told you, or until you ask to be removed, whichever comes first.
  • App data: for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your profile, logs and meal photos.
  • Error reports and usage data: kept for a limited time and then deleted by our providers.

8. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws, you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it
  • correct or update it
  • ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent
  • nominate someone to act for you
  • raise a complaint with us, and with the Data Protection Board of India if you’re not satisfied with our answer

You can delete your account and all its data from the app’s settings. To leave the waitlist, reply “remove” to any of our emails. For anything else, email crew@claww.me.

9. Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access limited to your own account. Meal photos are private and shared only through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects you.

10. Children

CLAWW is for people aged 18 and over. If you’re under 18, you may use CLAWW only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. If you believe a child has given us data without that permission, contact us and we’ll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

12. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email crew@claww.me with the subject “Privacy”. We aim to reply within 7 days.
